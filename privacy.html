Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

1. Introduction

FRC Team 10332 Chargebotics ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use the Forge training platform.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the site.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Register for a member account
  • Complete training modules in Forge
  • Submit forms or communicate with us
  • Participate in team activities

This information may include:

  • Name and display name
  • Email address
  • Team role and subteam affiliation
  • Training progress and completion status
  • Profile information and preferences

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URLs and pages visited
  • Time and date of access
  • Session duration and usage patterns

2.3 Training and Performance Data

For Forge platform users, we collect:

  • Quiz scores and assessment results
  • Module completion status
  • Training session duration
  • Video watch completion
  • Safety certification status

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and manage your member account
  • Training Compliance: Track and verify completion of required safety and technical training
  • Team Operations: Coordinate team activities, events, and communications
  • Safety Requirements: Ensure compliance with safety protocols and shop access requirements
  • Service Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve platform functionality
  • Communications: Send important team announcements and updates
  • Administrative Purposes: Process approvals and manage access permissions

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Within the Team

Your information may be shared with team mentors, coaches, and student leaders as necessary for team operations, training oversight, and safety compliance.

4.2 FIRST Robotics Competition

We may share limited information with FIRST to comply with competition requirements and registration processes.

4.3 Service Providers

We use third-party services including Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage. These providers have their own privacy policies and are bound by data protection agreements.

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of FRC Team 10332, team members, or others.

4.5 Public Information

Information displayed on public pages (team member names, photos, roles) is visible to anyone visiting our website. Member-only content remains restricted to authenticated users.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Secure authentication via Firebase
  • Access controls and permission levels
  • Regular security assessments

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Many of our team members are under 18 years old. We take special care to protect minors' privacy:

  • Parental consent is obtained for members under 13
  • We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from minors
  • Parents/guardians may request access to or deletion of their child's information
  • Photos and videos of minors are only published with appropriate consent

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to retention requirements)
  • Portability: Request a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@frc10332.org

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Training records may be retained for compliance and historical purposes.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use browser local storage to maintain your login session and training progress. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can clear local storage through your browser settings, but this may affect functionality.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as FIRST Robotics, social media platforms, and YouTube). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, when appropriate, providing additional notice.

12. California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Right to delete personal information (subject to exceptions)
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

13. International Users

Our services are provided from the United States. If you access our website from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where data protection laws may differ from your country.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

FRC Team 10332 Chargebotics
Email: admin@frc10332.org
Or through our official team communication channels

Your Consent: By using our website and services, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
